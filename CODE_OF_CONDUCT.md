# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our Pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information, such as real names, email addresses, or local file paths, without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Comments, issues, and pull requests that violate them may be edited, hidden, locked, or removed, and repeat offenders may be blocked from the repository.

## Reporting

To report a conduct problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the offending comment, issue, or pull request. For problems that cannot be reported that way, open a private report through the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/shallow-products/obsidian-format-convert-copy/security) and mark it as a conduct issue.

All reports are treated with discretion and respect for the reporter's privacy.
